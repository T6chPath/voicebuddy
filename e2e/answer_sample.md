**Photosynthesis** is how green plants make their own food: they use sunlight to turn water and carbon dioxide into sugar, and release oxygen.

It happens mainly in the leaves, inside tiny parts of the cell called **chloroplasts**. These contain **chlorophyll**, the green pigment that captures light energy.

## How it works

1. **Light is absorbed** — chlorophyll traps energy from sunlight.
2. **Water is split** — the energy breaks water into hydrogen and oxygen; the oxygen leaves through tiny pores.
3. **Sugar is built** — hydrogen and carbon dioxide from the air are joined into glucose.

The whole process in one line:

$$6CO_2 + 6H_2O \xrightarrow{\text{light}} C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2$$

## What plants need

| Needs | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Sunlight | The sun (or a grow lamp) |
| Water | Roots, from the soil |
| Carbon dioxide | Air, through the leaves |

> **Example:** A money plant on a sunny windowsill grows faster than one in a dark corner — it gets more light, so it can make more food.

> **Tip:** Remember it as *"light + water + air → food + oxygen"*.

> **In short:** Plants are tiny solar-powered kitchens — they cook sugar from water and air, and give us the oxygen we breathe.
